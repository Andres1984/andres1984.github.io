Academic Events

Events are also a way of building academic community, connecting institutions, and creating spaces where data science becomes a shared research language.

A selection of events that reflect my commitment to research formation, collaboration, and the growth of applied data science communities.

October 2023

First Data Science Meeting

Organized at Universidad Externado de Colombia, this event helped strengthen the academic identity of the undergraduate Data Science program and opened a shared space for dialogue between students, faculty, and research interests.

2024

StatDataHUB

Developed at Universidad Externado de Colombia in collaboration with Universidad Santo Tomás, this event created an academic meeting point between statistics, data science, and applied research.

2025

MeetUP Bayes-IA-ML

A collaborative initiative with Universidad de La Sabana, Universidad del Rosario, Universidad de La Salle, Escuela de Ingeniería de Antioquia, and the Bayes Plurinacional Community, centered on Bayesian methods, AI, and machine learning.

April 17, 2026

Encuentro de Semilleros de Investigación Aplicado a Ciencia de Datos

Organized at Universidad Externado de Colombia, this event is focused on student research, early-stage projects, and the role of data science in applied inquiry across disciplines.
